# Monetization & Business Model

## 1. Business principle

Do not put basic medication reminders behind a paywall if doing so undermines trust or makes the product feel exploitative.

Monetize:
- convenience;
- advanced scheduling;
- sync;
- caregiver;
- reporting;
- multi-profile workflows.

## 2. Consumer business hypotheses

### Free
Possible:
- 1 active treatment plan;
- unlimited medications within reasonable limits;
- basic meal-linked rules;
- local notifications;
- recent history;
- core Today timeline.

### Pro
Possible:
- multiple active plans;
- advanced dependencies;
- cycles/tapering;
- unlimited history;
- cloud backup;
- multi-device;
- caregiver;
- PDF reports;
- travel/timezone tools;
- multiple profiles;
- premium widgets.

Do not lock safety-critical explanation behind Pro.

## 3. Pricing hypotheses

Test, do not assume:

- €2.99–€4.99 monthly;
- €24–€39 annual;
- €59–€99 lifetime.

Regional pricing required.

A temporary-treatment user may prefer:
- 3-month pass;
- lifetime;
- free core.

Potential test:
**Treatment Pass — 90 days**

This aligns with temporary therapies better than indefinite subscription.

## 4. Subscription psychology

The product may face a natural objection:

> “Why do I need another subscription for an app that reminds me of pills?”

Therefore:
- value must clearly exceed reminders;
- lifetime option can improve trust;
- annual pricing should be modest;
- free core should remain functional.

## 5. Revenue scenarios

Use model, not fantasy.

Variables:

```text
monthly_new_users
activation_rate
paid_conversion
monthly_price_net
retention_months
store_fee
support_cost
infrastructure_cost
```

Example formula:

`MRR ≈ active_paid_users × net_monthly_ARPU`

Build scenarios:
- conservative;
- base;
- strong.

Do not use market-size reports as revenue forecasts.

## 6. Business channels

### B2C
App Store / Play Store.

### B2C caregiver
Family subscription.

### B2B2C future
Potential:
- clinics;
- pharmacies;
- pharma patient-support programs;
- insurers.

B2B can create:
- compliance burden;
- customization;
- sales cycle;
- data-governance requirements.

Do not pursue before consumer product is strong unless opportunity is unusually compelling.

## 7. Clinician-facing value later

Potential:
- treatment-plan template sharing;
- adherence report;
- schedule instructions;
- patient handoff.

This could be valuable because clinicians often provide timing instructions that users then manually operationalize.

However, clinician features may change regulatory and enterprise requirements.

## 8. Pharmacy channel later

Potential:
- QR code with schedule template;
- pharmacist configures timing instructions;
- user imports into app.

Requires robust source/authenticity model.

## 9. Cost structure

MVP low:
- app stores;
- developer accounts;
- crash/analytics;
- optional backend;
- domain;
- legal/privacy/regulatory consultation.

Later:
- support staff;
- encrypted storage;
- security audits;
- clinical/regulatory;
- localization;
- insurance;
- B2B sales.

## 10. Unit-economics caution

Healthcare-adjacent support can be expensive.

A €2.99 subscription with high-touch support is weak.

Therefore design:
- excellent self-service;
- clear error explanations;
- diagnostic export;
- automated billing support;
- strict boundary between app support and medical advice.

## 11. Ethical monetization rules

Do not:
- sell medication/health data;
- run behavioral ads using health data;
- create fear-based upsells;
- paywall access to one’s own treatment history;
- imply Premium is safer.

Possible:
- charge for storage/sync;
- advanced scheduling;
- caregiver network;
- exports;
- multiple profiles.

## 12. Business validation

Before payments:
- ask willingness-to-pay in interviews;
- use fake-door “Pro” feature interest;
- test landing-page pricing;
- offer beta users a future price survey.

After payments:
- analyze by treatment duration.

Temporary users may convert differently from chronic users.

## 13. Potential pricing personas

### Temporary complex
Best:
- free;
- 90-day pass;
- lifetime.

### Chronic
Best:
- annual.

### Caregiver family
Best:
- annual family plan.

### Professional
Later:
- per-seat / per-patient / enterprise contract.

## 14. Business success target

Early goal should not be “€10k MRR.”

First prove:
1. users rely on the schedule;
2. they return;
3. a meaningful subset values advanced features enough to pay.

Revenue follows product trust.
