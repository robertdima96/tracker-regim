# Go-to-Market & Marketing

## 1. Marketing problem

“Medication reminder” is a commodity message.

DoseFlow needs to dramatize the specific pain:

> Doing arithmetic around medication and meals.

## 2. Primary positioning

Recommended:

> **Stop doing medication math.**

Support:

> DoseFlow turns your treatment instructions into a live schedule and updates linked times when your day changes.

## 3. Message pillars

### Pillar 1 — Dynamic
> Took something late? Your linked schedule updates.

### Pillar 2 — Meal-aware
> Know when you can eat based on the timing rules you configured.

### Pillar 3 — Explainable
> See why every time was calculated.

### Pillar 4 — Your instructions
> The schedule follows the treatment instructions you enter.

### Pillar 5 — Private
> Core planning works locally without requiring an account.

## 4. Avoided claims

Do not advertise:
- medically optimizes;
- guarantees correct treatment;
- prevents adverse effects;
- safer than your doctor;
- replaces professional advice.

## 5. Landing page structure

### Feature

### Feature

### Feature

- no medical advice;
- private by design;

### FAQ

- Does it support before/after meals?
- What happens when I miss a dose?
- Does it work offline?
- Android/iPhone?

## 9. Reddit/community strategy

Participate, do not spam.

Research communities where people discuss:
- GERD/gastric treatment;
- chronic illness;
- medication adherence;
- caregivers;
- shift work;
- fertility treatment;
- app/productivity.

Post format:
- describe problem;
- show prototype;
- ask if workflow resonates;
- disclose developer relationship.

## 10. Beta recruitment

Initial:
20–50 users.

Aim for diversity:
- 2–3 medications;
- 6+ daily events;
- meal-dependent;
- actual-relative intervals;
- variable schedules.

Recruit via:
- personal network;
- Reddit;
- niche communities;
- product-building communities.

## 11. Referral loop

Potential later:

> Share DoseFlow with someone managing a complicated treatment.

Do not incentivize with medication-related streaks.

## 12. Product-led growth

Exports can create soft awareness:
- clinician report branded subtly;
- shared caregiver invite;
- treatment template link.

No dark patterns.

## 13. Launch sequence

### Pre-alpha
Dogfood.

### Closed beta
Invite only.

### Public beta
Limited countries/languages if support capacity is low.

### v1 launch
App Store / Play Store + focused content campaign.

### Product Hunt
Optional. It may generate developer interest but may not be the best patient-acquisition channel.

## 14. Localization

Start:
- English;
- Romanian if founder can support it well.

Later based on demand:
- German;
- Spanish;
- French;
- Italian.

Medication names and reference databases vary by country; keep schedule engine country-agnostic.

## 15. Brand tone

- calm;
- precise;
- non-judgmental;
- adult;
- supportive but not cute;
- no hospital bureaucracy;
- no mascots required.

Avoid:
> “Oopsie! You missed your pill 😢”

Prefer:
> “This dose has not been logged.”

## 16. Marketing metrics

- landing page signup rate;
- cost per beta signup;
- App Store page conversion;
- activation after install;
- organic search acquisition;
- channel-specific retention;
- referral rate;
- treatment completion.

## 17. Launch risks

### Risk
People interpret “When can I eat?” as medical advice.

Mitigation:
Marketing and UI say:
> “Based on the timing rules you configured.”

### Risk
Message is too complex.

Mitigation:
Use a visual before/after demo instead of explaining “constraint graph.”

### Risk
App looks useful only for GI medication.

Mitigation:
Use GI as wedge examples but show generic dependencies and interval examples.
